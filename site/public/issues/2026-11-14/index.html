13 NovGitHubBonus

4,837 hijacked GitHub accounts planted a CI secret stealer

From 10 November the accounts probed 61,764 repositories, ran a 1,500-line script in CI, then wiped the logs. Aikido confirmed stolen secrets from 1,835 targets.

The campaign, which Aikido calls wfx-loot, started at about 12:45 UTC on 10 November. Each account opened a pull request titled “docs: dependency audit notes” to test for write access, and 92% of those pull requests are still open. Where it could write, the attacker changed a GitHub Actions workflow, added one on a short-lived branch, or planted an npm install hook, then reverted the change and deleted the runs and logs in under a minute. The script takes Actions secrets, GITHUB_TOKEN and OIDC tokens, AWS, Azure and Google Cloud credentials, npm and PyPI tokens, and keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Cursor and GitHub Copilot, and it trades OIDC tokens for cloud sessions. The hijacked accounts include ones at Fortune 100 companies, the US General Services Administration, the French government, Nextcloud and WebdriverIO, and about 50 of them can publish packages. Automated publishing already carried the payload into a Packagist package and a Go module. Look for a wfx-docs branch, the probe pull request and gaps in workflow run numbers. Where the payload ran, rotate every secret and cloud role that the workflow could reach.

10 NovARC RaidersBonus

ARC Raiders turns one with 17 million sold, as sales slow

Only 1 million came in the third quarter. Nexon expects fourth-quarter revenue to fall against last year's launch and stay “broadly flat sequentially”.

Embark Studios' extraction shooter came out at the end of October 2025. Nexon's latest earnings report says it has sold more than 17 million copies, only 1 million of them in the third quarter. Nexon expects ARC Raiders revenue in the fourth quarter to fall against the launch quarter a year ago, but called the revenue so far “within the outlook”. It named the Frozen Trail update as a key moment that brought back some lapsed players and introduced new ones. The game remains one of Nexon's most successful titles: the company mainly credits it for its revenue growth in the nine months to 30 September. The open question is PvE. After a successful test, Embark has not said whether PvE returns as a permanent feature, though the Chinese version already has a dedicated PvE mode. Frozen Trail also lifted the game's Steam player count, and reports suggest it helped ARC Raiders reach a six-month player high across platforms. Executive producer Aleksander Grøndal said he could see PvP and PvE modes co-exist in the future.
