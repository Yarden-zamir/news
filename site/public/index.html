Trust issues, all week

21 OctSecurity Extra!

Eight stolen certificate keys, and a week of patches

Private keys of eight eMudhra intermediate certificates were compromised. Browsers flipped a switch. Tools with their own trust lists shipped updates.

The certificates chain to roots that Mozilla, Microsoft, Apple and Chrome trust, so whoever holds the keys can forge a certificate for any domain. Cloudflare learned of it on 19 October and deployed its mitigations a day later; cloudflared users need 2026.10.2. In Python, uv 0.13.4, pip 26.2.2 and certifi 2026.10.19 ship new certificate lists. Docker Compose, the Slack CLI and T3 Code followed with Go, Deno and Electron updates. Cloudflare's advice: do not fix the errors by turning verification off.
